# Technology research

Follow section 15 of the Enterprise ERP AI Engineering Master Guide:
1. List missing requirements before recommending.
2. Compare at least three options using current official documentation.
3. Score with the approved weights (business fit 20, security 20, maintainability 15, reliability 15, team 10, performance 10, cost 10).
4. Separate verified facts, estimates and judgment; cite sources with dates. Never invent prices, benchmarks or rankings.
5. Name what needs a proof of concept, security review, performance test or human decision.
6. Draft an ADR with /write-adr.
